THE BRIEF

Start with
the right idea.

The goal: Turn scattered posts into a recognizable, repeatable content system.


The challenge: Posting more does not automatically create a stronger brand. Without visual and editorial rules, a feed can feel disconnected.


The creative direction: AFTERHOURS is a fictional lifestyle concept used to demonstrate a modular social system: a consistent type scale and palette, with enough flexibility for different messages and campaign moments.
