THE BRIEF

The goal: Create a flexible product visual language that feels premium and consistent.


The challenge: Product content often relies on repetitive pack shots. This direction creates a more ownable mood while keeping the product at the center.


The creative direction: FORME is a fictional fragrance label used to demonstrate a visual system: warm neutrals, sculptural shadows, close detail and confident typography. It is a speculative concept, not a commissioned campaign.
