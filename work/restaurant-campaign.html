THE BRIEF

Start with
the right idea.

The goal: Make a local food brand feel instantly recognizable across the feed.


The challenge: Restaurant promotions can become a wall of prices and menu items. This concept gives the food a mood first, then makes the offer easy to understand.


The creative direction: EMBER is a fictional neighborhood restaurant concept with a warm, high-contrast visual system: deep charcoal, ember orange, cream typography and tight food crops. The work is a demonstration of direction and execution, not a claim of client results.
