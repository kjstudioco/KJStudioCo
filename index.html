08 GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
answered.

Clear expectations make for better creative.
Here is what to know before we start.

Are the listed prices fixed?

They are starting prices. Before we begin, your proposal will spell out the deliverables, formats, timeline and revision scope so everyone knows what is included.

Can I book one project instead of a monthly plan?

Absolutely. You can start with a single post, Reel, product visual, ad creative or brand project. Monthly support is there when you need a more consistent flow.

Do you publish content or manage social accounts?

Creative-only projects are available, and ongoing social management can be scoped separately depending on your platforms and needs.

Do you use AI to make the work?

We use modern tools, including AI, to explore ideas and work efficiently. Creative direction, brand fit, editing and quality checks remain central to the process. We sell considered creative work, not a tool-generated shortcut.

How do we get started?

Send a short note about your business, what you need and your timeline. We'll reply to discuss fit, scope and next steps before any work begins.
