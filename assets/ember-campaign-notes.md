# EMBER — Campaign Creative Notes

**Type:** Self-initiated fictional concept by KJStudioCo, not commissioned client work. No performance claims are made.

## Creative platform
**Good food. No quiet nights.**

EMBER is imagined as a neighborhood kitchen with late-night energy. The visual language pairs charcoal-black backgrounds, ember orange, warm cream typography and oversized food imagery.

## Production checklist
1. Replace illustrative visuals with original, licensed, or client-approved photography.
2. Replace fictional brand, tagline, CTA and menu language with approved business details.
3. Confirm offers, dates, prices, address and ordering method before publishing.
4. Export approved artwork as PNG/JPG and review on a phone.
5. Suggested Reel: close-up → texture/detail → hero plate → tagline → CTA.

## Design rationale
- Strong contrast and short headlines support fast reading.
- A repeatable palette and footer tie feed and story formats together.
- The brand is fictional; this is a demonstration concept, not a client result.
